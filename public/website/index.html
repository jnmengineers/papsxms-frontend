About us

A Seventh-day Adventist school offering the Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) in a caring, disciplined and Christ-centred environment.

Our mission

To nurture every learner academically, spiritually, physically and socially, preparing them for service in this life and the life to come.

Adventist values

Integrity, respect, love for God and others, hard work and responsibility — lived out every day in class and beyond.

Why choose us

Dedicated teachers, small classes, regular assessment and clear progress reports for every learner.

Academics

We follow the Kenyan CBC curriculum from the early years through Junior School.

Pre-Primary

Play Group, PP1 and PP2 — language, numeracy, creativity and early life skills through play.

Lower Primary

Grades 1–3 — strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, Kiswahili and integrated learning areas.

Upper Primary

Grades 4–6 — sciences, agriculture, creative arts, social studies and religious education.

Junior School

Grades 7–9 — integrated science, pre-technical studies, agriculture and more, preparing for senior school.
